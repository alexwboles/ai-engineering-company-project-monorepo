# HealthCore operating brief

Source: `CONTEXT.md` and `CONTEXT_HEALTHCORE.md` in this repository.

## Company and footprint

HealthCore is an outpatient healthcare network founded in Austin, Texas in 2011. It operates 12 clinics: nine in the United States across Texas, Florida, and Georgia, and three in the United Kingdom across London and Manchester. The network provides primary care, specialist consultations, chronic disease management, and preventive health programmes. HealthCore has approximately 200 employees and HealthCore Digital is the internal technology unit responsible for modernising operational workflows.

## Departments and ownership

Clinical Operations is led by Dr. Marcus Reid and coordinates physicians, nurse practitioners, nurses, and medical assistants across the 12 clinics. Patient Experience and Access owns booking, appointment reminders, follow-up, and the patient journey. Revenue Cycle and Billing owns commercial insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, private pay, and NHS billing. Compliance and Data Governance owns HIPAA in the United States and UK GDPR in the United Kingdom. People and Workforce owns hiring, onboarding, compliance training, and continuing medical education. Technology owns integration across legacy EHR, billing, scheduling, and operational systems. Executive Leadership, led by Dr. Sandra Okonkwo, needs comparable weekly operating reports.

## Operational priorities

HealthCore currently reports a 22% network no-show rate, a 14% claim denial rate, and an average of 47 days to close a clinical hire. Clinical staff spend approximately 35 minutes per day on documentation tasks. These figures are operational signals: they should be interpreted by the responsible department and tied to a measurable improvement action rather than presented as isolated dashboard numbers.

## Services and records

The company provides primary care, chronic disease management, preventive care, specialist care, women's health, paediatric care, and mental health services. Core operational records include claims, appointments, clinicians, and locations. Claims have a claim ID, patient ID, clinic location, service type, payer, submission date, amount, status, denial reason when denied, and resubmission flag. Appointments have an appointment ID, patient ID, clinic location, service type, scheduled date and time, status, and an optional no-show reason. Clinicians have a clinician ID, role, assigned location, licence expiry date, and CME requirement and progress. Locations include the clinic, city, country, contact details, and average consultation fees.
